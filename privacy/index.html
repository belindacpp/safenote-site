随时记—加密笔记本 / SafeNote — Notes & Diary

更新日期 / Last updated: 2026-10-12

中文

随时记—加密笔记本无需注册账号。笔记、图片、涂鸦、未完成草稿和安全设置保存在你的设备上；App 不会把这些内容上传到开发者服务器，也不包含广告或跨 App 跟踪。

你可以手动生成带恢复码的加密备份，再自行选择保存到“文件”或其他位置。若选择第三方文件服务，其数据处理由该服务负责。开发者无法读取你的备份内容，也无法找回应用锁密码或备份恢复码。丢失恢复码、删除 App 或设备损坏可能导致数据无法恢复。

相机只在你选择拍照时使用；照片由系统选择器提供；Face ID 或 Touch ID 只用于本机快捷解锁。生物特征数据不会提供给开发者。拒绝这些权限不影响基础文字记录。

你可以在 App 内删除单篇笔记或笔记本。卸载 App 会删除设备上的 App 数据；已导出的备份文件需在保存位置另行删除。App 不提供云账号或云同步。

如需支持，可在 App 的“设置 → 问题反馈与支持”中打开系统邮件 App。发送邮件由你主动决定；邮件内容和地址会由你的邮件服务及收件方处理。请勿发送应用锁密码、恢复码或笔记中的敏感内容。隐私问题可联系 belinda101012@gmail.com。

English

SafeNote does not require an account. Notes, images, drawings, unfinished drafts, and security settings are stored on your device. The app does not upload this content to the developer's servers and contains no advertising or cross-app tracking.

You can manually create an encrypted backup protected by a recovery code and choose where to save it using the system Files interface. If you choose a third-party file service, that service handles the file under its own privacy practices. The developer cannot read your backup or recover your app passcode or recovery code. Losing the code, deleting the app, or losing the device may make data unrecoverable.

The camera is used only when you choose to take a photo. Photos are selected through the system picker. Face ID or Touch ID is used only for quick on-device unlock; biometric data is not provided to the developer. Denying these permissions does not prevent basic text notes.

You can delete individual notes and notebooks in the app. Uninstalling the app removes its on-device data. Exported backup files must be deleted separately from wherever you saved them. The app does not offer cloud accounts or cloud sync.

For support, open the system Mail app from Settings → Feedback and Support. Sending an email is your choice; your mail provider and the recipient process its address and content. Do not include passcodes, recovery codes, or sensitive note content. For privacy questions, contact belinda101012@gmail.com.
